# Lab-03: Life cycle and methodology · Realtime Quiz

The Realtime Quiz project utilizes a **Hybrid life cycle** combining structured predictive stage gates (G1–G4) with iterative 2-week Scrum delivery sprints. This approach bridges the sponsor's requirement for a locked-down plan and approved milestone schedule by week 3 with the flexibility needed to handle high technical uncertainty regarding WebSockets and real-time synchronization.

The project sponsor maintains rigorous control through four formal stage-gate reviews and strict stage tolerances across time, cost, scope, and quality. Daily scrums, sprint reviews, and weekly highlight reports ensure transparent progress tracking while protecting the final Go-Live date.
